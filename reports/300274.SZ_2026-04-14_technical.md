## 9. Conclusion

Sungrow Power Supply (300274.SZ) is exhibiting a classic **oversold bounce pattern** following the Q4 2025 earnings shock. The short-term technical picture has improved markedly over the past 5 trading sessions, driven by exceptionally strong institutional capital inflows and an aggressive recovery in price (+9.8% in 5 days).

However, the stock remains in a **structurally bearish position** from a medium-to-long-term perspective. All major moving averages (MA20 through MA200) are above the current price, the MACD is still negative, and the RSI has not yet reclaimed the 50 level. The ADX at 58 confirms the downtrend remains powerful.

The key question for traders and investors is whether this bounce represents:
- **(A) A dead-cat bounce** that will stall near 140-148 before resuming the downtrend, or
- **(B) The beginning of a genuine bottoming process** that will eventually lead to a trend reversal

The capital flow data strongly favors scenario (B), as the magnitude and persistence of institutional buying (+15.94B over 5 days) is inconsistent with a mere short-covering rally. The most likely near-term path is continued recovery toward the 145-150 zone, where the stock will face its first serious technical test against the MA20 and the earnings gap.

**Technical Verdict: Neutral-to-Cautiously-Bullish for the short term (1-2 weeks), Bearish for the medium term (1-3 months) until confirmed above MA50 (~160).**
