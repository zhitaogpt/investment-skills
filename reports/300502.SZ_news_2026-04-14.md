## 1. Major Company News Events (Past 1-3 Months)

### 1.1 Extraordinary General Meeting (April 13, 2026)

Eoptolink held its 2026 First EGM on April 13, 2026, passing all resolutions with overwhelming approval (up to 99.98%):

- **Board expansion:** The board of directors is increased from 7 to 8 members, adding 1 employee representative director. This is directly linked to the equity incentive plan — 187 employees received 5.6 million shares (post-split) with a cost basis of just CNY 16.168/share. With the stock at CNY 520, aggregate paper gains exceed CNY 2.8 billion (avg ~CNY 15M per person). The new director seat ensures employee/incentive-holder representation at the board level.

- **Related-party transaction with Hongchen Optoelectronics (鸿辰光电):** Eoptolink forecasts up to CNY 1.5 billion in procurement from Chengdu Hongchen Photon Semiconductor Technology Co. in 2026. Hongchen is a related party (founded by the same controlling shareholders) that has grown into one of China's leading optical chip companies with annual capacity of 150 million chips. This vertical integration into silicon photonics chip supply is a strategic move but warrants monitoring for related-party pricing fairness.

- **Charter amendments:** Updates to registered capital and business scope reflecting the company's expansion.

NOTE: The stock already trades at CNY 520, above Goldman's CNY 518 target, indicating the market has fully priced in Goldman's expectations.

### 1.3 OFC 2026 Product Showcase (March 12-16, 2026)

Eoptolink made a significant impression at the OFC 2026 exhibition with multiple cutting-edge products:

- **1.6T DR4 OSFP transceiver:** Based on 400G/lambda IMDD (Intensity Modulation Direct Detection) technology with integrated (8:4) PAM-4 DSP, converting 8x200G electrical lanes to 4x400G optical lanes
- **12.8T XPO solution:** Next-generation ultra-high-bandwidth product for hyperscale data centers
- **6.4T NPO solution:** Mid-range next-gen product
- **NX200/NX300 OCS products:** Optical Circuit Switch products that improve GPU interconnection efficiency while reducing power consumption and latency

The OFC showcase confirms Eoptolink's positioning at the technology frontier, with product roadmaps extending well into the 3.2T and beyond era.

## 2. Industry and Competitive Dynamics

### 2.1 Global Optical Module Market: Explosive Growth Continues

According to LightCounting's March 2026 update:

- **2025 global sales:** ~USD 18 billion, +70% YoY
- **2026 forecast:** +60% growth expected, market approaching USD 29 billion
- **2031 forecast:** ~USD 60 billion (under neutral scenario)
- **Key driver:** Hyperscale AI/ML data center buildouts by Google, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon; 800G is in full-scale deployment; 1.6T entering commercial volume production

### 2.2 Technology Transition: 800G to 1.6T to 3.2T

The industry is at a critical inflection point:

- **800G:** Full-scale volume production in 2026; Eoptolink's 800G market share has been rising rapidly (estimated ~20% share in 400G/800G combined by Q3 2025 per Cignal AI)
- **1.6T:** Entering commercial production in 2026, with mass volume expected in 2027; silicon photonics share at 1.6T estimated at ~20%
- **3.2T:** In foundational R&D stage; product architectures being defined
- **CPO (Co-Packaged Optics):** Eoptolink ranks globally #2 with ~15-18% market share

### 2.3 Competitive Landscape: "Yi Zhong Tian" (易中天) Three Giants

The Chinese optical module industry is dominated by three companies ("Yi Zhong Tian"):

| Company | Global Share | Key Strength | 2025-26 Status |
|---------|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Zhongji Innolight (中际旭创)** | 25-30% (#1 globally) | Scale, broadest customer base, 14-15M units 800G capacity | Market cap ~CNY 800B+ |
| **Eoptolink (新易盛)** | 15-18% (#2 globally) | Fast growth, vertical integration (Hongchen chip), deep Google/Meta ties | Market cap ~CNY 519B |
| **Tianfu Communications (天孚通信)** | Components leader | Optical components, upstream supply | Market cap significant |

Since 2025 through April 13, 2026, Eoptolink and Innolight have each risen approximately 9.5x from their lowest points, while Tianfu Communications has surged ~800%.

### 2.4 Emerging Competitors and Moat Concerns

A bearish narrative is emerging around Eoptolink's competitive moat:

- **Huilv Ecology (001267.SZ):** Originally a landscaping company, entered optical modules via acquisition of Wuhan Junheng Technology; now covers 10G-800G and developing 1.6T; stock up 478% since 2025
- **Zhaochi (002429.SZ):** LED company pivoting to optical communication; completed 400G/800G reliability testing, entering small-batch production; developing 1.6T; stock up 82%
- **Research spending concern:** Eoptolink's R&D expense was only CNY 2.01B in 2024 and CNY 5.01B for 9M 2025 — relatively low for its scale, though rapidly growing in absolute terms

However, key barriers remain: Eoptolink's supply chain integration with hyperscalers (Google, Meta), its Hongchen silicon photonics vertical integration, and the capital/certification requirements to enter Tier-1 cloud vendor supply chains provide meaningful competitive protection.
