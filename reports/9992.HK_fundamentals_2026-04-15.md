## Part 3 -- Summary

### 4. Growth Quality Assessment: Is 185% Revenue Growth Sustainable?

**Short answer: No, and the market knows it.**

- FY2025 growth of 185% was driven by three non-repeatable factors: (a) LABUBU global viral explosion, (b) massive new store openings (+109 stores globally), and (c) Americas market entering from near-zero base (+748%). [Source: GoogleSearch multiple sources]
- Management itself guided only 20%+ growth for 2026, implying a deceleration from 185% to approximately 20-30%. This is a massive step-down. [Source: GoogleSearch "泡泡玛特 2026年目标增速不低于20%"]
- Analyst consensus expects 2026 revenue of RMB 45.5 billion (+22.6%). [Source: simplywall.st]
- Dividend payout ratio was cut from 35% (2024) to 25% (2025), which Morningstar analyst Jeff Zhang flagged as a "negative signal." [Source: GoogleSearch "wallstreetcn 泡泡玛特"]

**The key question is not whether growth slows, but whether it stabilizes at 15-25% (a very healthy rate for a consumer company with 72% gross margins) or collapses below 10% as LABUBU fades.**

Structural supports for sustained mid-teens+ growth:
1. Overseas penetration is still early -- only 185 overseas stores vs. 445 in China. The US market went from $0 to ~$1 billion in one year; even at a slower pace, ongoing expansion can sustain 20%+ overseas growth.
2. IP pipeline is active: 17 IPs generating >RMB 100 million, and "Star People" scaling from RMB 120 million to RMB 2.06 billion demonstrates new IP incubation capability.
3. Category expansion from figurines to plush, derivatives, and eventually theme parks provides multiple growth vectors.
4. Member base of 72.6 million with 55.7% repeat purchase rate creates a flywheel.

Key risks:
1. LABUBU at 38.1% revenue concentration -- any fade in this IP without an equivalent replacement would be material.
2. Counterfeit products and secondary market price compression may erode brand cachet.
3. Consumer discretionary nature means macro sensitivity (especially in China).
4. Overseas execution risk as the company scales rapidly across 20 countries.
