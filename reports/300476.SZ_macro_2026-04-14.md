## 2. Monetary Policy & Financing Environment

**PBOC stance: Moderately Loose -- Most accommodative since 2008-2009.**

PBOC Governor Pan Gongsheng confirmed at the NPC session (March 6, 2026) that the central bank will "flexibly and efficiently use RRR cuts, interest rate reductions, and other monetary policy tools" throughout 2026. In January 2026, PBOC already cut structural lending facility rates by 25bps and expanded the Sci-Tech relending facility from 800B to 1.2T CNY. [Source: GoogleSearch "中国 货币政策 降准 降息 2026年 PBOC"]

**Transmission to Shenghong Technology:**

- **Financing cost reduction:** Shenghong announced a 2026 capex plan of up to 20B CNY (fixed assets + R&D), the most aggressive expansion in its history. With further RRR/rate cuts expected in H2 2026, the company's borrowing cost for this massive expansion will be structurally lower. Each 25bp rate cut on a 10B CNY debt base saves approximately 25M CNY annually.
- **Valuation support:** Lower risk-free rates compress the discount rate for high-growth tech companies. At the current 260B CNY market cap, a 50bp reduction in WACC could justify a 10-15% valuation uplift, all else equal.
- **Sci-Tech relending:** The expanded 1.2T CNY Sci-Tech facility specifically targets companies in advanced manufacturing and core technologies -- PCB for AI servers directly qualifies.

**Assessment: Strongly Favorable.**

## 3. Industrial Policy Analysis

### 3.1 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-2030) -- "Extraordinary Measures for IC"

The 2026 Government Work Report explicitly calls for "extraordinary measures to advance key core technology breakthroughs across the full chain of integrated circuits, industrial mother machines, and advanced materials." PCB, as the foundational substrate for all IC packaging and systems, is a direct beneficiary. Local governments (Shenzhen, Nantong, Guangde) have rolled out specific PCB industry cluster policies with tax incentives and land subsidies. [Source: GoogleSearch "十五五规划 电子信息 PCB 集成电路"]

### 3.2 National IC Fund Phase III ("Big Fund III")

Big Fund III officially launched in January 2026 with a total scale exceeding 350B CNY, with the first tranche of 120B CNY already deployed. While primary targets are semiconductor equipment and wafer fabs, the supply chain linkage to PCB (especially advanced substrates and HDI for IC packaging) creates indirect policy capital flow. [Source: GoogleSearch "大基金 PCB 投资 半导体 2026"]

### 3.3 AI Computing Infrastructure Policy

China's "East-to-West Computing" strategy and data center build-out continue to accelerate. By 2025, China reached 1,590 EFLOPS of smart computing capacity with 42 10K-card clusters built. The 2026 outlook forecasts over 500B CNY in dedicated computing infrastructure funds. Each AI server requires 3-5x the PCB content value of a traditional server, with high-layer-count boards (20+ layers) and HDI technology -- precisely Shenghong's core competency. [Source: GoogleSearch "AI服务器 政策 数据中心 算力 中国 2026"]

**Transmission to Shenghong Technology:**

- **Direct order pipeline:** Shenghong is a direct NVIDIA supplier for AI server PCBs. The company's 2025 revenue surged 79.8% to 19.29B CNY, driven largely by AI server PCB orders. Policy-driven computing infrastructure expansion ensures sustained demand through 2027+.
- **Capex validation:** The company's 20B CNY 2026 capex plan (focusing on HDI, substrate-like PCB, and high-multilayer boards) is directly aligned with policy priorities, potentially qualifying for government subsidies and preferential land/tax treatment.
- **Global PCB market reaching $958B in 2026** (+12.5% YoY) with AI infrastructure as the #1 growth driver validates the industry tailwind. [Source: GoogleSearch "PCB行业 产业政策 扶持 2026"]

**Assessment: Strongly Favorable.**

## 4. US-China Trade & Tariff Risk

**Current situation: Elevated tension, but PCB not directly targeted -- yet.**

The US imposed a 25% tariff on semiconductor imports and semiconductor manufacturing equipment effective January 15, 2026 (Section 232). However, the tariff specifically covers semiconductors and their "derivative products" with an 18-month grace period for certain categories (deferred to mid-2027). PCBs are currently classified separately from semiconductors and are NOT included in the 25% semiconductor tariff. [Source: GoogleSearch "中美贸易 关税 电子元器件 2026年4月"]

TechInsights estimates that if tariffs escalate to 30-40% across broader electronics categories, the global semiconductor market could contract by up to 34%. While this is a worst-case scenario, it highlights systemic risk.

**Transmission to Shenghong Technology:**

- **Direct tariff exposure: LOW.** Shenghong's primary customers are in mainland China, with products shipped to OEMs/ODMs domestically. The finished servers (not PCBs) are the exported end-product. PCBs are not individually classified under current tariff regimes.
- **Indirect supply chain risk: MODERATE.** If US tariffs on Chinese electronic end-products escalate further, downstream OEMs could slow orders. However, the "domestic substitution" trend actually benefits Shenghong -- as Chinese OEMs seek to localize their supply chains, Shenghong gains share from Taiwanese/Japanese PCB competitors.
- **NVIDIA supply chain restructuring:** Any potential US restrictions on NVIDIA's China-specific AI chips (like the H20) could temporarily impact Shenghong's AI server PCB orders. This remains the single largest geopolitical risk.

**Assessment: Moderate Risk (manageable, but requires monitoring).**

## Conclusion

The macro environment for Shenghong Technology is **strongly favorable** across nearly all dimensions. China's most accommodative monetary policy in 15+ years directly reduces the financing cost of the company's unprecedented 20B CNY capex plan. The 15th Five-Year Plan's "extraordinary measures" for IC and electronics, the 350B CNY Big Fund III, and the >500B CNY AI computing infrastructure buildout collectively create a policy super-cycle for high-end PCB demand. The HK IPO timing (April 21, 2026) coincides with peak market receptivity, with A+H dual-listing momentum at historic highs.

The principal macro risk remains US-China trade escalation -- specifically, any expansion of semiconductor tariffs to cover PCB substrates or any new restrictions on NVIDIA's China-market AI chips. These risks warrant close monitoring but currently represent tail risks rather than base-case scenarios.

**Macro Rating: 8.4/10 -- Strongly Favorable with manageable geopolitical tail risks.**
