**Sentiment Intensity Score: 6/10**

## 2. Key Discussion Topics and Viewpoints

### 2.1 The Q4 2025 "Earnings Shock" — Central Theme Across All Platforms

The dominant narrative across Xueqiu, Eastmoney Guba, and financial media centers on the severe Q4 2025 earnings miss:
- **Q4 revenue:** 22.78B CNY, -18.37% YoY
- **Q4 net profit:** 1.58B CNY, -54.02% YoY
- **Q4 gross margin:** Collapsed to ~23%, down 13 percentage points QoQ

**Xueqiu** (April 4): A widely-shared deep dive post described the crash as driven by "four overlapping negatives": Q4 earnings shock, export tax rebate cancellation, HK IPO valuation suppression, and institutional mass selling. The post noted "classic pattern of institutions dumping while retail investors catch the falling knife" — main force funds net outflowed 2.78B CNY on April 1 while retail net inflowed 1.15B CNY.

**Eastmoney Guba** (April 1-10): Posts titled "Near four-year first quarterly revenue-profit double decline" and "The bright side and hidden worries behind full-year growth" dominated the discussion board, reflecting widespread concern about the Q4 deterioration.

### 2.2 The Bull Case — "Dawn Before Darkness"

A significant bullish contingent has emerged, particularly after the stock dropped below 130 CNY:

- **Eastmoney Guba** (April 11): A widely-read post titled "Three Core Investment Opportunities, Enormous Valuation Recovery Space" argued that at ~2567B market cap (as of April 10), the forward PE of ~14x on 2026 estimates places the stock at the bottom 10th percentile of its 5-year valuation history. Analyst target prices cited: Goldman Sachs 162.8 CNY, CITIC Securities 190 CNY, Soochow Securities 185 CNY — implying 31%-53% upside.

- **Xueqiu** (April 8): A deep value analysis post gave a "Buy" rating with a 12-month target of 180-200 CNY, arguing the stock is at a "critical inflection point — short-term pressure, long-term positive." Key bull arguments: energy storage shipment target of 60+ GWh in 2026 (+40% YoY), AIDC business potentially reaching 50B CNY revenue by 2027, and Mexico factory coming online Q3 2026 to bypass US trade restrictions.

- **Xueqiu** (March 11): An earlier post argued the stock was "severely undervalued at 20x PE" and that "45-60x PE matches its sector positioning, target price 500-700 CNY" — though this extreme bull case appears to be a minority view.

### 2.3 The Bear Case — Structural Risks Remain

Bear arguments are still prominent:

- **ZAKER News** (April 9): Article titled "Sungrow: From 'White Horse Stock' to 'Grey Rhino'" — arguing the Q4 miss signals deeper structural issues, not a one-time blip.

- **Eastmoney** (April 8): A critical article highlighted that senior executives (Vice Chairman Gu Yilei, SVP Wu Jiamao, VP Deng Dejun, VP Wang Lei) collectively sold 753,400 shares worth over 120M CNY between August-December 2025 — just before the stock crashed from its November high of ~210 CNY. The article questioned whether Haitong International's bullish coverage (target price 206 CNY, issued January 2026) was irresponsible for not flagging insider selling as a key risk.

- **Export Tax Rebate Cancellation**: Effective April 1, 2026, the 9% VAT export rebate for 249 solar products was fully eliminated. With overseas revenue at 60.54% (539.92B CNY in 2025), this is estimated to erode 15-20% of net profit. This remains a key bearish anchor.

### 2.4 Company Guidance for 2026 — The Counter-Narrative

Management provided aggressive 2026 guidance during the March 31 investor call:
- **Revenue:** 1,100-1,200B CNY
- **Net profit:** 170-190B CNY (+26% to +41% YoY)
- **Energy storage shipments:** 60+ GWh (+40% YoY)
- **Q1 2026:** Company explicitly stated "Q1 results will show a clear reversal"

This forward guidance has been a key support for the bullish camp, with Eastmoney Guba posts frequently citing it as evidence the bottom is in.

**Net Assessment:** Institutional sentiment is **divided** — long-term capital (Northbound, insurance) has been accumulating on weakness, while index/trend-following funds reduced exposure. Sell-side remains majority "Buy" but with lowered target prices (down 10-20% from pre-crash levels).

### 5.1 The April 1 Capitulation Event
The -10.82% single-day crash on 225.4B CNY turnover (10.49% turnover rate) represented a classic capitulation event. The extreme volume suggests exhaustive selling — when both institutions and retail who wanted to sell had done so.

### 5.2 The April 7 Low (~122 CNY) as Potential Bottom
- RSI(6) reached 22 — deep oversold territory
- Stock traded at ~12% percentile of 5-year PE range
- The subsequent 5-day recovery of +14% from the low suggests the 120-125 CNY zone may represent a durable support level

### 5.3 April 14 Breakout Signal
Today's +3.89% advance on +1.23B main force net inflow, with the stock reclaiming 139 CNY, is potentially significant. The super-large order dominance (+1.376B) suggests this is not retail-driven momentum but institutional positioning. If the stock holds above 135 CNY, the sentiment regime may have shifted from "bear rally" to "recovery trend."

### 5.4 Key Risk: HK IPO Overhang
The pending Hong Kong listing (filed October 2025, 6-month validity window) continues to suppress A-share valuation. Market expects H-share pricing at 90-105 HKD (~83-97 CNY), requiring 25-35% AH discount. This structural overhang will persist until the IPO is completed or withdrawn.

## 8. Conclusion

Sungrow Power Supply's market sentiment is at a **critical inflection point**. The April 1 crash (-10.82%) on record volume represented a capitulation event that flushed out weak holders. Since the April 7 low of ~122 CNY, the stock has recovered 14% to 139.46 CNY, driven by a notable shift in fund flows — institutional/main force capital has turned from aggressive sellers to strong accumulators, with April 14's super-large order inflow of +1.38B CNY being the most decisive signal.

The sentiment landscape remains divided: retail forums show passionate debate between "golden pit" bulls and "structural decline" bears, while sell-side analysts have universally maintained Buy ratings (albeit with 10-20% target price cuts). The most actionable signal is the **reversal in the institutional-retail flow dynamic** — institutions are now net buyers while retail takes profits, which historically precedes sustained recovery rallies.

Key risks that prevent a higher sentiment score: (1) the unresolved HK IPO overhang creating a structural valuation ceiling, (2) the April 1 export tax rebate cancellation with uncertain profit impact, and (3) insider selling in the months prior to the crash suggesting management may have anticipated the Q4 miss.

The weight of evidence points to sentiment bottoming and a recovery trend forming, with the 120-125 CNY zone likely representing durable support. However, a decisive break above 150 CNY — the pre-crash institutional selling zone — would be needed to confirm the sentiment regime has fully shifted to bullish.
