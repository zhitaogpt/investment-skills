## 1. Major Company News Events (Past 1-3 Months)

### 1.1 FY2025 Annual Report: Record Revenue, but Q4 "Thunderbolt"

On March 31, 2026, Sungrow released its FY2025 annual report. Full-year results were record-breaking:
- **Revenue:** CNY 891.84B (+14.55% YoY)
- **Net Profit:** CNY 134.61B (+21.97% YoY)
- **EPS:** CNY 6.55
- **Gross Margin:** 31.83% (+1.89ppt YoY)
- **Operating Cash Flow:** CNY 169.18B (+40.18% YoY), significantly exceeding net profit growth

However, **Q4 2025 was a disaster** that overshadowed the full-year results:
- **Q4 Revenue:** CNY 227.82B (-18.37% YoY, -0.38% QoQ)
- **Q4 Net Profit:** CNY 15.80B (-54.02% YoY, -61.9% QoQ)
- **Q4 Gross Margin:** 22.95% (down 4.53ppt YoY, down 12.92ppt QoQ from Q3's 35.87%)
- **Q4 Deducted Net Profit:** Down -59.5% YoY

The full-year results actually **missed consensus expectations** -- 22 analysts had estimated revenue of CNY 971.71B and net profit of CNY 153.33B (per Wind data). Actual figures fell short by ~8% on revenue and ~12% on profit.

**Root causes of Q4 collapse:**
1. **ESS quantity-up, price-down squeeze:** Q4 ESS shipment volume rose 40% QoQ to 14GWh, but ASP crashed from ~CNY 1.1/Wh to ~CNY 0.6/Wh -- nearly halved. ESS gross margin dropped ~17ppt QoQ to ~24%.
2. **Lithium carbonate price spike:** Battery-grade lithium carbonate surged from ~CNY 77K/ton (Sep 2025) to >CNY 120K/ton by Dec 2025 (~66% quarterly increase), the largest quarterly gain since 2023. Cell costs (>45% of ESS system cost) rose sharply.
3. **Revenue mix shift:** Low-margin new energy development projects concentrated delivery in Q4, raising its revenue share by ~10ppt, dragging overall margins.
4. **Asset impairment charges concentrated in Q4:** Of the CNY 21.85B total impairment provisions for FY2025, ~40% (CNY 9.04B) was recognized in Q4 alone (credit impairment CNY 4.79B +702% QoQ, asset impairment CNY 4.25B +61% QoQ).

The market reaction was severe: **the stock fell 10.82% on April 1** (day after release), evaporating >CNY 300B in market cap in a single day. Over 3 trading days (Apr 1-3), the stock shed ~CNY 540B total.

### 1.2 Management Guidance for 2026: Strong Recovery Expected

At the March 31 earnings call, management issued surprisingly strong 2026 guidance:
- **Revenue target:** CNY 1,100-1,200B (+23% to +35% YoY)

- **ESS shipment target:** 55-70 GWh (+28% to +63% YoY)
- **AIDC power revenue target:** CNY 10-15B (scaling up from near-zero)
- **Q1 2026:** Management explicitly stated "clear reversal" with orders for both ESS and inverters recovering significantly QoQ

Key strategic highlights from the earnings call:
- Overseas ESS orders booked into Q2 2026, with capacity utilization at 92%
- North America: AWS 8GWh framework order; Google/Meta to ramp deliveries in 2026
- **Self-developed ESS cells** to begin mass production in Q2 2026, with costs 18% below external procurement
- Gross margin guidance: "Stable or slightly improving"
- Dividend policy: 25% of net profit (CNY ~9.50/share for FY2025 declared, ex-dividend record already set)

## 2. Industry and Competitive Dynamics

### 2.1 Global Inverter Market: Dominant Position Maintained

Per Frost & Sullivan data (published Feb 2026):
- Sungrow holds **~25.2% global PV inverter market share**, ranking #1 worldwide for **10 consecutive years**
- Sungrow + Huawei together dominate ~80% of newly installed inverters in Europe (per ECFR report, Mar 31, 2026)
- FY2025 inverter revenue: CNY 311.36B (+6.9%), global shipments: 143GW (-2.72%)
- Domestic inverter shipments fell from 70GW to 57GW as the company strategically abandoned negative-margin projects
- Overseas inverter shipments grew ~12% YoY, slightly above market growth

### 2.2 Global Energy Storage: Explosive Growth but Margin Compression

**Market size (2025):** Global lithium battery ESS installations reached 317 GWh (+74% YoY):
- China: 178 GWh (+82%), accounting for >56% of global new additions
- Americas: +46% YoY
- Europe: +71% YoY
- Asia-Pacific: +92% YoY

**Sungrow's ESS performance:**
- FY2025 ESS revenue: CNY 372.87B (+49.39%), overtaking inverters as the #1 business segment (41.81% of revenue)
- ESS shipments: 43 GWh (+54%), but below the global market growth rate of 74%
- **Domestic ESS shipments declined** from 9 GWh to 7 GWh (management intentionally withdrew from unprofitable domestic projects)
- **Overseas ESS shipments surged** from 19 GWh to 36 GWh (+90%), now >83% of total ESS shipments
- Domestic ESS market share dropped from ~10% to ~5%

**Pricing pressure is severe:**
- CNESA data: 2h ESS system average bid price fell from ~CNY 650/kWh to ~CNY 554/kWh (-14.41% YoY in 2025)
- 4h ESS system average bid price fell -26.07% YoY
- Sungrow's Q4 ESS ASP crashed to ~CNY 0.6/Wh (from ~CNY 1.1/Wh in Q3)
- Industry-wide, "below-cost bidding" has become a norm in some domestic projects

**Competitor landscape:**
- CATL (300750.SZ), BYD (002594.SZ) are accelerating overseas ESS expansion, intensifying competition
- Haibo Sichuang (海博思创): FY2025 revenue CNY 116B (+40%), but also acknowledged Q4 domestic margin compression
- Kelü Electronics (科陆电子): Q4 swung to net loss of CNY 3.88B
- The domestic ESS market is in a brutal price war; overseas markets remain higher margin but competition is spreading

### 2.3 Trade Policy and Tariff Risks

- **US tariffs:** Current tariffs on Chinese solar/battery products remain elevated. Bloomberg (Jan 28, 2026) noted "Sungrow can withstand US tariffs in the near term as higher US market prices should allow it to remain profitable."
- Sungrow has a **3 GW inverter manufacturing facility** option for localized production to mitigate tariff impacts
- **EU concerns:** ECFR report (Mar 31, 2026) flagged European dependency on Chinese inverters (Huawei + Sungrow dominate 80% of new installations), raising potential security/policy scrutiny
- **Export tax rebate reduction:** Starting April 1, 2026, China reduced export tax rebates for PV panels from 13% to 9%, adding cost pressure

### 2.4 AIDC (AI Data Center) Power -- Emerging Growth Driver

Sungrow is positioning to capture the AI datacenter power opportunity:
- **Products:** Solid-state transformers (SST), 800V high-voltage DC power solutions (98.5% efficiency, >15% energy savings)
- **Customers:** Passed certification from Amazon (AWS), Microsoft, and other hyperscalers
- **2026 target:** CNY 10-15B revenue from AIDC power business
- **Industry context:** AI computing demand is projected to create ESS demand equivalent to "three US markets" (per Jiemian, Mar 18, 2026)
- SST prototype completed; small-scale commercial delivery expected H2 2026

### 2.5 Wind Power Converter: Global #1

Per Brinckmann data, Sungrow holds **34% global market share** in wind power converters, ranking #1 worldwide. This is a smaller but stable business contributing to diversification.
