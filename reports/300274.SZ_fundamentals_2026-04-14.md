## 1. Company Overview and Business Model

Sungrow Power Supply Co., Ltd. (阳光电源, 300274.SZ) is the world's largest solar inverter manufacturer and a rapidly scaling energy storage systems integrator, headquartered in Hefei, Anhui Province, China. Founded in 1997, the company listed on the Shenzhen ChiNext board in 2011.

**Core Business Segments:**

- **Solar Inverters** (~35% of FY2025 revenue): String inverters for utility-scale, commercial, and residential solar installations. Sungrow is the global #1 by shipment volume, competing with Huawei Digital Power (unlisted), Sineng Electric, and international players like SMA Solar and Enphase Energy.

- **Energy Storage Systems** (~42% of FY2025 revenue): Battery energy storage systems (BESS) for utility-scale, C&I, and residential applications. This segment overtook inverters as the #1 revenue contributor in FY2025, growing 49.4% YoY to approximately CNY 37.3 billion.

- **New Energy Development & Other** (~23% of FY2025 revenue): EPC services, floating PV systems, EV charging, hydrogen, and AI data center power solutions.

**Geographic Mix:** Approximately 60.5% of FY2025 revenue came from overseas markets (Europe, Americas, Asia-Pacific, Middle East & Africa), providing diversification but also exposing the company to trade policy risks (tariffs, export VAT rebate changes).

**Competitive Moats:**
- Scale advantage: 891.8 billion CNY revenue base enables aggressive R&D spending (~5% of revenue) and cost leadership
- Vertical integration across inverters, BESS, and EPC
- Strong brand recognition in 170+ countries
- Installed base of 605 GW+ globally, creating recurring service revenue potential
- Policy tailwinds from China's NDRC Document No. 114 (Jan 2026), establishing capacity pricing for independent energy storage

**Is the discount justified?**

Sungrow trades at a massive discount to peers on every valuation metric. Part of this discount is deserved due to:
1. Q4 2025 earnings shock that damaged market confidence
2. Higher revenue base (CNY 892B vs. peers at CNY 80-122B) limiting relative growth rate
3. Trade policy headwinds (tariffs, VAT rebate cancellation) disproportionately affecting Sungrow's large export base

However, the discount appears excessive given:
1. Sungrow's ROE (31.3%) is the highest among listed peers
2. 2026 guided earnings growth of 26-41% is among the strongest
3. Operating cash flow conversion (OCF/NI = 1.26x) is excellent
4. Balance sheet is healthy (current ratio 1.67, manageable leverage)
5. Goodwill is minimal at CNY 2.97B (0.46% of assets)

A "justified" PE for Sungrow, given its ROE of 31% and guided growth of ~34%, would be approximately 25-28x forward earnings among China growth equities. This implies significant undervaluation at the current 21.3x trailing PE.

## 6. Key Risks

1. **Guidance Execution Risk:** The 2026 revenue target of CNY 1,100-1,200B requires 23-35% growth after a weak Q4. If Q1 2026 does not show meaningful recovery, the stock could re-rate downward.

2. **Lithium Carbonate Cost Pressure:** Battery-grade lithium carbonate prices surged to ~CNY 170K/ton in early 2026, compressing BESS margins. Since storage is now 42% of revenue, this is a material headwind.

3. **Trade Policy:** China's export VAT rebate cancellation (effective April 1, 2026) adds 6-9% cost pressure on overseas revenue, which accounts for 60.5% of total.

4. **Competitive Intensity:** Huawei Digital Power (unlisted) is an aggressive competitor in inverters; new entrants in BESS (CATL, BYD) could compress margins further.

5. **Customer Concentration and Receivables:** 103-day receivable turnover days suggest extended payment terms, typical for utility-scale projects but raising working capital risk.
