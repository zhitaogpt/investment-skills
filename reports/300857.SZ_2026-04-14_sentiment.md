## 2. Industry Competitive Landscape Assessment

**AI Server and Computing Power Leasing Market (2026):**

The global AI server market reached USD 125.1 billion in 2024 and is projected to grow rapidly, with AI intelligent computing demand exhibiting a 49% CAGR over the next three years. [Source: GoogleSearch "AI服务器 算力 行业展望 2026", 搜狐/知乎]

The industry is transitioning from a "resource scarcity-driven" model to a "quality and efficiency-driven" model in 2026, with four structural trends reshaping the ecosystem: (1) supply-demand rebalancing, (2) inference workload explosion, (3) domestic substitution acceleration, and (4) computing-power-and-electricity coordination. [Source: GoogleSearch "AI服务器 算力 行业展望 2026", 搜狐]

**Sharetronic's positioning**: The company has carved out a differentiated niche in "AI computing power leasing services" -- a high-value-added subsegment within the broader supply chain. Its four business pillars (intelligent computing products/services, data storage, server remanufacturing, AIoT) create vertical integration advantages. The April 2026 acquisition of Guangwei Technology extends this into optical chips/modules, a critical bottleneck in data center interconnects. [Source: GoogleSearch "协创数据 行业分析 AI服务器", 21财经/163.com]

**Key competitors** in the server remanufacturing space include Apto Solutions and other fragmented players globally. In domestic AI computing leasing, the company competes with major cloud providers and specialized computing operators, but its scale of investment (CNY 110B+ in server procurement) positions it as one of the most aggressive capital deployers in the sector. [Source: GoogleSearch "协创数据 行业分析 AI服务器", 公司2024年年报]
