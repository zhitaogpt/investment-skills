- **Effective January 15, 2026:** The US imposed **25% tariffs on select semiconductor imports** under Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962.

- **Assessment:** This adds cost pressure to any optical module containing US-bound semiconductor components, but Eoptolink's Thailand manufacturing strategy (discussed below) substantially mitigates this risk.

## 4. International / Geopolitical Factors

### US-China Trade Tensions and Tariff Regime
- **China reciprocal tariffs:** As of April 2026, the US-China tariff regime remains elevated. The US maintains broad tariffs on Chinese goods, with the effective tariff rate on Chinese exports to the US remaining among the highest globally.
- **Optical module specific risk:** Chinese-origin optical modules exported directly from China to the US face substantial tariff headwinds. This is why Eoptolink and peers (e.g., Zhongji InnoLight) have strategically built **overseas manufacturing capacity in Thailand**.

### Thailand Tariff Arbitrage -- Eoptolink's Key Advantage
- **US-Thailand reciprocal tariff agreement:** Thailand faces a **19% tariff** on goods exported to the US (significantly lower than China's tariff rate). Products that are merely transshipped through Thailand (without substantial manufacturing) face a punitive **40% tariff**.
- **Eoptolink's Thailand operations:**
  - Phase I and Phase II of Eoptolink's Thailand factory are **both fully operational** as of 2026.
  - **Phase III** was brought forward to Q1 2026, with **1.6T monthly capacity exceeding 500,000 units** and overseas production now representing **over 50% of total capacity**.
  - The company performs **substantial manufacturing** in Thailand (not mere transshipment), which qualifies products for Thailand-origin treatment under US rules.
  - **Eoptolink also has a Singapore subsidiary**, creating a multi-jurisdiction structure that further optimizes tariff and tax efficiency.
- **Thailand export data (Q1 2026):** Data from Thai customs shows active optical module and PCB exports from Thailand to North America in January-March 2026, confirming the manufacturing-and-export pipeline is functioning.
- **Assessment:** Eoptolink's Thailand strategy is a **critical competitive moat** in the current tariff environment. The 19% Thailand tariff rate (vs. potentially 60%+ for direct China-to-US exports) preserves the company's ability to serve North American hyperscaler customers profitably. The Phase III acceleration demonstrates management's proactive approach to capacity planning.
